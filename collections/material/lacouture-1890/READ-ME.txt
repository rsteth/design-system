CHARLES LACOUTURE — REPERTOIRE CHROMATIQUE (PARIS, 1890)

This collection includes all 29 distinct chromolithographed plates, numbered I,
I bis, and II–XXVIII, plus 10 supplementary scanned pages. The opening rose
frontispiece duplicates Plate I and is not a thirtieth distinct chart.

Images are full-page 3000-pixel-high JPEG conversions of the processed JP2
images supplied by the Getty Research Institute via Internet Archive. They
preserve the photographed paper, printing, page edges, and photographic color.
No color correction, white balance, or cropping has been applied.

The full-size archive uses high-quality JPEG encoding. The compact website
archive uses stronger JPEG compression at the same pixel dimensions. Both
are derivatives, not lossless masters. The original JP2 and TIFF archives
remain available at the primary source below.

SUPPLEMENTARY MATERIAL
Original front wrapper; opening rose; printed rose guide, recto and verso;
title page; atlas title; both atlas contents pages; rear wrapper/publisher
advertising; original viewing screen. An inserted modern English dealer
description at scan index 224 is not included as original 1890 material.

COMPLETENESS
The companion full book PDF preserves all 226 scanned pages, including the
French explanatory text and its line diagrams, blank versos, and inserts.
The image inventory identifies each selected source leaf (zero based) and
PDF page (one based).
