## Charles Lacouture · Répertoire chromatique (1890)

The 29 plates and 10 supplementary pages in `collections/material/lacouture-1890/scans/` are uncorrected 3000px-high JPEG derivatives of Charles Lacouture’s 1890 work, chromolithographed by G. Severeyns, digitized by the Getty Research Institute via [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/gri_33125012875395). Internet Archive records `NOT_IN_COPYRIGHT`; [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Getty_Research_Institute_(IA_gri_33125012875395).pdf) documents public-domain status. Source pages and file hashes are retained in the material manifest. The supplied README and CSV inventory are retained unchanged. No cropping, white balance or color correction was applied here. These are scans, not calibrated ink measurements.

The separately linked web atlas is an authored digital interpretation, not a Getty publication. Its existing private access is unchanged; its private source is not distributed by this repository.
