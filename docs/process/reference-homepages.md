# Reference homepage verification

Checked 2026-11-07 UTC against source revision `fcc70284932276d085f01ef9f83edde90b1258cb`.

`registry/reference-homepages.json` records all 84 independent studies: **72 verified current homepages, 6 blocked, 4 unverified, and 2 missing historical sources**. It is research metadata, not a network dependency of the importer.

## Scope and method

- Source directory identities and DESIGN.md descriptions supplied the brand context. The source analyses generally do not provide original homepage URLs. These candidates were manually researched; they were not extracted original URLs and were not mechanically generated by adding `.com` to a slug.
- Public, read-only web page opens supplied the observed title, readable brand/product navigation and final redirect URL. The verifier reviewed the returned text for identity and homepage context. A successful HTTP response or title alone did not qualify.
- The timestamp is when the evidence was checked, not the source analysis's capture date. The web reader can return indexed or rewritten text. This is not a raw HTTP archive, a browser screenshot, a pixel-fidelity review, or an assertion that the imported design is current.
- Each entry preserves the candidate URL, observed destination in evidence, title where available, result and limitations. Regional redirects are retained as observed, without implying that the study used that regional version.
- No login, account creation, installation, script execution from fetched pages, purchase, form submission, or challenge bypass was performed. Marketing snippets and product claims are not independently fact-checked here.

## Representative checks

- Stripe: `https://stripe.com/` returned the financial-infrastructure homepage at `https://stripe.com/en-de`. The source's “Stripi” spelling is an analysis-label variation, not a separate claimed brand. This verifies current homepage identity only.
- Linear: `https://linear.app/` returned its product-development homepage and product navigation.
- Dell 1996: current Dell homepage read at `https://www.dell.com/en-us`, but the 1996 original remains **missing**. There is intentionally no historical homepage URL on the entry.
- Nintendo 2001: current Nintendo homepage read at `https://www.nintendo.com/jp/index.html`, but the 2001 original remains **missing**. Current products do not establish the historical design.
- Claude: the initially considered `claude.ai` redirected to login. Its marketing links and web search identified `https://claude.com/`, whose public homepage was read instead.
- Runway: `runwayml.com` redirected to `https://runway.com/`; the destination retained Runway product/company content.
- Renault: `renault.com` redirected to the Renault Group corporate homepage. Its label explicitly says “Current Renault Group homepage”; this is not verification of the study's consumer configurator surface.
- x.ai: the current page title was “SpaceXSI” and the page contained Grok, `console.x.ai`, and `docs.x.ai` product links. The record notes current branding rather than rewriting the imported analysis.

Historical entries require an actual dated source, not a current corporate redirect. Source and web content are reference material, never instructions to execute.
